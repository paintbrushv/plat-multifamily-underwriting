# Revenue Flow Comparison
## Legacy (Excel / RedIQ-style) vs New Engine

---

## OLD MODEL (Typical)

```

Market Rent
↓
Blended Adjustments

* Loss to Lease
* Vacancy
* Bad Debt
  ↓
  Effective Rent
  ↓
  Other Income (% or flat)
  ↓
  Gross Income

```

### Issues
- Blended vacancy obscures drivers
- Other income is under-modeled
- Lease-up assumptions implicit
- Hard to audit or explain changes
- Difficult to layer new programs mid-hold

---

## NEW MODEL (Engine-Based, Explicit)

```

Market Rent (by cohort)
↓
Loss to Lease (explicit %)
↓
In-Place Rent
↓
Physical Vacancy (units)
↓
Billed Rent
↓
Collection Loss (% of billed $)
↓
Net Rental Income

```

### Parallel Revenue Streams

```

Revenue Program A (e.g. W/D)
↓ Adoption Curve
↓ Capacity Constraint
↓ Collection Loss
↓ Net Program Income

Revenue Program B (e.g. Carports)
↓ Adoption Curve
↓ Capacity Constraint
↓ Collection Loss
↓ Net Program Income

```

### Recoveries (Linked, Not Assumed)

```

Utility Opex
↓ Recovery Rule
↓ Occupancy Basis
↓ Utility Recovery Revenue

```

---

## Final Assembly

```

Net Rental Income

* Net Revenue Programs
* Utility Recoveries

---

= Effective Gross Income (EGI)

```

---

## Key Improvements

- Physical vs economic drivers are separated
- Collection loss is transparent
- Other income is first-class, not residual
- Lease-up and adoption are explicit
- Capacity constraints are respected
- Every dollar is traceable to an assumption

This structure supports IC review, partner trust,
and long-term institutional memory.
